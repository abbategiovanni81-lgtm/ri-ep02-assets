# DEX AI TOOLS · EP01 · Script v2 (retention cut)
## "I Sent Claude ONE Message. It Made a Whole Cartoon."

**Runtime:** about 6:30 (around 1,050 words of narration at Dex's pace, plus the demo film)
**Host:** Dex, in the black DEX AI TOOLS hoodie, in his studio
**Running gag:** Dex's DEX mug. He makes a coffee when he presses Start, and an on-screen coffee timer counts down until the cartoon is ready. It gives the episode a ticking clock without leaning on the reference video's devices.

### How to read this script
- **SHOT** = what's on screen. Panel codes are from your Dex images (key at the end).
- **TEXT** = on-screen graphics. **Green** words = the neon-green highlight.
- **DEX** = what Dex says.
- `[RE-HOOK]` = a new reason to keep watching (roughly every 45 to 60 s).
- `[INTERRUPT]` = a visual or sound pattern break (roughly every 15 to 20 s).
- Change what's on screen every 3 to 5 s, even during talking-head shots: punch-in, angle change, cutaway, or a graphic.

### Fill these in from your own run before recording
| Tag | What to put in |
|---|---|
| `[CREDITS]` | Flow credits your video actually used |
| `[TIME]` | Minutes from Start to finished video |
| `[MISTAKE]` | One real thing that went wrong while you built it |
| `[CH1] [CH2]` | Real channels you screenshot, with only the stats shown on their page that day |
| `[FREEBIE]` | What you'll actually send people who comment |
| `[NEXT]` | Next episode's topic |
