# DEX — "Where Does the Internet Actually Live?"

**Runtime:** 5:00 · **Beats:** 40 · **Sheets:** 5 × 8 panels
**Production method:** storyboard-first (see `method/STORYBOARD-METHOD.md`)
**Host:** Dex, following one message from a phone to the other side of the world.

VO ≈ 700 words @ 140wpm. Each beat ≈ 7.5s. Keep VO lines short — the picture
carries the load, the line only names what the picture can't say.

---

## SEQUENCE 1 — THE QUESTION (0:00–1:00)

**1 · 0:00–0:08 — DEX SENDS A MESSAGE**
VO: "Dex types four words to a friend on the other side of the planet. Hits send."
ACTION: Thumb taps send. Message bubble pops up the screen.

**2 · 0:08–0:15 — IT'S ALREADY THERE**
VO: "It arrives before he puts the phone down. Eight thousand miles, gone in a blink."
ACTION: Delivered tick appears. Dex blinks.

**3 · 0:15–0:23 — WHERE DID IT GO?**
VO: "But it went somewhere. Something physical carried it. So — where?"
ACTION: Dex looks up. Question mark rises.

**4 · 0:23–0:30 — "THE CLOUD"**
VO: "Everyone says the cloud. Which sounds soft, and far away, and above us."
ACTION: Cartoon cloud drifts in over Dex's head.

**5 · 0:30–0:38 — THE CLOUD IS A LIE**
VO: "There is nothing in the sky. Not one part of this happens up there."
ACTION: Cloud pops. Dex unimpressed.

**6 · 0:38–0:45 — IT'S BUILDINGS AND CABLE**
VO: "The internet is concrete, copper, glass and seawater. It has an address."
ACTION: Cloud's shadow resolves into a data centre silhouette.

**7 · 0:45–0:53 — FOLLOW IT**
VO: "So let's do the only sensible thing. Let's follow the message."
ACTION: Dex points forward. Determined.

**8 · 0:53–1:00 — SHRINK DOWN**
VO: "Dex gets very, very small — and climbs aboard."
ACTION: Dex shrinks, hops onto the message bubble like a raft.

---

## SEQUENCE 2 — PHONE TO EXCHANGE (1:00–2:00)

**9 · 1:00–1:08 — INTO RADIO**
VO: "First the message stops being words. It becomes a radio wave leaving the phone."
ACTION: Bubble dissolves into a wave, launches off the antenna.

**10 · 1:08–1:15 — THE TOWER CATCHES IT**
VO: "A mast a few streets away catches it. That's the whole job of a cell tower."
ACTION: Wave arcs across rooftops into the mast.

**11 · 1:15–1:23 — DOWN THE MAST**
VO: "Straight down the tower into a grey cabinet nobody has ever looked at twice."
ACTION: Dex slides down the mast into the cabinet.

**12 · 1:23–1:30 — INTO THE FIBRE**
VO: "And from the cabinet, into a glass thread thinner than a hair."
ACTION: Cabinet door opens on a glowing fibre bundle.

**13 · 1:30–1:38 — NOW IT'S LIGHT**
VO: "The message changes again. Now it's pulses of light. On, off, on, off."
ACTION: Dex surfs a light pulse down the fibre.

**14 · 1:38–1:45 — UNDER THE ROAD**
VO: "Under the pavement, under the traffic, under everyone's feet."
ACTION: Cutaway — street above, fibre duct below.

**15 · 1:45–1:53 — THE LOCAL EXCHANGE**
VO: "It surfaces in a windowless building at the end of an ordinary road."
ACTION: Dex emerges into a hall of blinking equipment.

**16 · 1:53–2:00 — SORTED AND SENT**
VO: "Here it gets read, addressed, and pushed onward. Half a millisecond, tops."
ACTION: Routing lights flick. Dex flung down the next fibre.

---

## SEQUENCE 3 — THE DATA CENTRE (2:00–3:00)

**17 · 2:00–2:08 — ARRIVAL**
VO: "Next stop is the thing people actually mean when they say 'the cloud.'"
ACTION: Vast shed on a flat site. Dex arrives at the fence.

**18 · 2:08–2:15 — THE SCALE OF IT**
VO: "A shed the size of a town centre. No windows. No signage. Enormous."
ACTION: Camera pulls back — Dex is a speck.

**19 · 2:15–2:23 — ROWS AND ROWS**
VO: "Inside, aisles of racks running away from you further than you can see."
ACTION: Dex walks a cold aisle. Perspective lines to infinity.

**20 · 2:23–2:30 — FINDING THE SERVER**
VO: "One of these machines, in one of these racks, is handling Dex's four words."
ACTION: A single rack unit lights up. Dex points.

**21 · 2:30–2:38 — IT LANDS ON A DISK**
VO: "The message stops being light and becomes a physical mark on a physical disk."
ACTION: Macro — pulse hits platter, mark written.

**22 · 2:38–2:45 — THE HEAT**
VO: "All of this makes heat. Absurd amounts of it. Most of the building is fans."
ACTION: Heat shimmer, giant cooling units, Dex's hair blown back.

**23 · 2:45–2:53 — AND THE POWER**
VO: "And if the power drops for one second, generators outside take over."
ACTION: Rank of generators, one thumping into life.

**24 · 2:53–3:00 — COPIED, AND ONWARD**
VO: "A copy is made. Then the message is thrown at the ocean."
ACTION: Duplicate bubble spawns, rockets out of frame.

---

## SEQUENCE 4 — THE OCEAN (3:00–4:00)

**25 · 3:00–3:08 — TO THE COAST**
VO: "Inland fibre runs to the shoreline, to a building even fewer people notice."
ACTION: Map-style dash from data centre to coast.

**26 · 3:08–3:15 — THE LANDING STATION**
VO: "A cable landing station. This is where the internet gets its feet wet."
ACTION: Small coastal building, cables entering the ground.

**27 · 3:15–3:23 — INTO THE SEA**
VO: "The cable leaves the beach, drops off the shelf, and keeps going."
ACTION: Cable snakes from sand into surf. Dex rides it under.

**28 · 3:23–3:30 — THINNER THAN YOU'D THINK**
VO: "Out in deep water the whole thing is about as thick as a garden hose."
ACTION: Cross-section — Dex holds the cable, hose-width.

**29 · 3:30–3:38 — REPEATERS**
VO: "Every sixty miles a repeater grabs the fading light and shouts it onward."
ACTION: Cylindrical repeater on the seabed pulses bright.

**30 · 3:38–3:45 — THE SEABED ROAD**
VO: "It crosses trenches, ridges and plains that no one has ever properly mapped."
ACTION: Wide seabed vista, cable running to the horizon.

**31 · 3:45–3:53 — HOW FRAGILE IT IS**
VO: "Anchors cut these. Trawlers cut these. It happens about two hundred times a year."
ACTION: Anchor drags nearby. Dex flinches.

**32 · 3:53–4:00 — THE FAR SHORE**
VO: "But not today. Today it makes it, and the seabed starts climbing."
ACTION: Cable rises toward light. Far shoreline ahead.

---

## SEQUENCE 5 — ARRIVAL (4:00–5:00)

**33 · 4:00–4:08 — LANDFALL**
VO: "Another beach. Another quiet building. Another country entirely."
ACTION: Cable exits surf into a mirror-image landing station.

**34 · 4:08–4:15 — INLAND AGAIN**
VO: "Back into buried glass, running under roads Dex will never see."
ACTION: Fibre route inland, new architecture flicking past.

**35 · 4:15–4:23 — ANOTHER EXCHANGE**
VO: "Another windowless hall. Another half-millisecond decision."
ACTION: Second exchange, routing lights flick.

**36 · 4:23–4:30 — LAST CABINET, LAST TOWER**
VO: "Grey cabinet. Up the mast. Back into thin air for the final few streets."
ACTION: Dex rockets up the mast, launches as a wave.

**37 · 4:30–4:38 — THE PHONE LIGHTS UP**
VO: "And a phone on a table, eight thousand miles away, buzzes once."
ACTION: Friend's phone lights. Message lands.

**38 · 4:38–4:45 — HOW LONG THAT TOOK**
VO: "Two exchanges, one data centre, one ocean. About a tenth of a second."
ACTION: Stopwatch snaps to 0.1s.

**39 · 4:45–4:53 — THE REAL MAP**
VO: "None of it was in the sky. All of it was in the ground and the water."
ACTION: World map draws itself — cables, not clouds.

**40 · 4:53–5:00 — THE CLOUD HAS AN ADDRESS**
VO: "The cloud is a shed with a postcode and a very large electricity bill."
ACTION: Dex back at full size, smug. Cloud icon replaced by a building.

---

## Notes for the animation pass

- Dex is the only continuous element across all 40 beats — keep his scale gag
  consistent: tiny from beat 8 to beat 39, full size only in 1–7 and 40.
- The message bubble is the second continuity thread. It changes form four times
  (text → radio → light → physical mark → light) and each change should read
  visually, not just in VO.
- Beats 18, 30 and 39 are the three "scale" shots. Give them the widest framing
  in the episode; everything else stays mid or close.
