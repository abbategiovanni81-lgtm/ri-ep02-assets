# DEX EP — Storyboard Sheet Prompts (5 sheets × 8 panels)

Paste one block per generation. The **STYLE BLOCK is identical in all five** —
do not reword it between sheets, that consistency is the whole point of the test.

Generate in order, approve sheet-by-sheet, re-roll the whole sheet if any single
panel fails. Slice to `sheetN_01.png … sheetN_08.png` for the animation pass.

---

## STYLE BLOCK (verbatim in every sheet prompt)

> Storyboard contact sheet, 8 panels in a 2×4 grid, landscape 1536×1024.
> Art style: handmade stop-motion claymation, visible fingerprints and thumb
> marks in the clay, soft macro depth of field, warm practical lighting, shallow
> tabletop miniature feel. Character "Dex": a small clay figure, round friendly
> head, oversized curious eyes, teal jacket, dark trousers — identical design and
> proportions in every panel he appears in.
> Panel furniture: numbered badge top-left of each panel (dark rounded square,
> white numeral); timecode chip beside it (amber background, dark mono text);
> caption bar across the bottom of each panel — deep navy, TITLE in bold white
> caps, body copy in centred white sentence case, ACTION line last in amber.
> Thin light gutters between panels. No other text anywhere in the image.

Swap the art-style sentence if Dex's channel look differs — but swap it in all
five sheets, and note the swap in the test log.

## SHEET 1 — THE QUESTION (0:00–1:00)

[STYLE BLOCK]

Panels:

1. `0:00-0:08` — **DEX SENDS A MESSAGE** — Dex at a clay table, thumb on a clay
   phone, message bubble popping up the screen.
   Caption: "Dex types four words to a friend on the other side of the planet."
   ACTION: Thumb taps send, bubble pops up.
2. `0:08-0:15` — **IT'S ALREADY THERE** — Same table, delivered tick on screen,
   Dex mid-blink.
   Caption: "It arrives before he puts the phone down. Eight thousand miles."
   ACTION: Tick appears, Dex blinks.
3. `0:15-0:23` — **WHERE DID IT GO?** — Dex looking upward, clay question mark
   rising above him.
   Caption: "Something physical carried it. So where did it actually go?"
   ACTION: Question mark rises and wobbles.
4. `0:23-0:30` — **"THE CLOUD"** — A fluffy clay cloud drifting in above Dex's head.
   Caption: "Everyone says the cloud. Soft, far away, and somewhere above us."
   ACTION: Cloud drifts in overhead.
5. `0:30-0:38` — **THE CLOUD IS A LIE** — The clay cloud bursting apart, Dex
   unimpressed, arms folded.
   Caption: "There is nothing in the sky. Not one part of this happens up there."
   ACTION: Cloud pops apart.
6. `0:38-0:45` — **CONCRETE AND CABLE** — The cloud's shadow on the ground
   resolving into the silhouette of a vast windowless shed.
   Caption: "The internet is concrete, glass and seawater. It has an address."
   ACTION: Shadow morphs into a building.
7. `0:45-0:53` — **FOLLOW IT** — Dex pointing forward, determined, jacket flapping.
   Caption: "So let's do the only sensible thing and follow the message."
   ACTION: Dex points forward.
8. `0:53-1:00` — **SHRINK DOWN** — Dex now tiny, standing on top of the glowing
   message bubble like a raft.
   Caption: "Dex gets very, very small — and climbs aboard."
   ACTION: Dex shrinks and hops on.

## SHEET 2 — PHONE TO EXCHANGE (1:00–2:00)

[STYLE BLOCK]

Panels:

1. `1:00-1:08` — **INTO RADIO** — Tiny Dex riding a wave leaving a clay phone's antenna.
   Caption: "First the message stops being words and becomes a radio wave."
   ACTION: Bubble dissolves into a wave, launches.
2. `1:08-1:15` — **THE TOWER CATCHES IT** — Wave arcing over clay rooftops into a
   cell mast.
   Caption: "A mast a few streets away catches it. That's a cell tower's whole job."
   ACTION: Wave arcs into the mast.
3. `1:15-1:23` — **DOWN THE MAST** — Dex sliding down the mast into a grey
   roadside cabinet.
   Caption: "Straight down into a grey cabinet nobody has looked at twice."
   ACTION: Dex slides down the mast.
4. `1:23-1:30` — **INTO THE FIBRE** — Cabinet door open on a glowing bundle of
   glass threads.
   Caption: "And from the cabinet, into a glass thread thinner than a hair."
   ACTION: Cabinet door swings open.
5. `1:30-1:38` — **NOW IT'S LIGHT** — Dex surfing a bright pulse down the inside
   of a fibre tube.
   Caption: "The message changes again. Now it's pulses of light. On, off, on."
   ACTION: Dex surfs the pulse.
6. `1:38-1:45` — **UNDER THE ROAD** — Cutaway diorama: clay street and traffic
   above, glowing fibre duct below.
   Caption: "Under the pavement, under the traffic, under everyone's feet."
   ACTION: Camera pushes down through the cutaway.
7. `1:45-1:53` — **THE LOCAL EXCHANGE** — Dex emerging into a windowless hall of
   blinking clay equipment racks.
   Caption: "It surfaces in a windowless building at the end of an ordinary road."
   ACTION: Dex emerges, lights blink.
8. `1:53-2:00` — **SORTED AND SENT** — Routing lights flicking, Dex flung toward
   the next fibre mouth.
   Caption: "Here it gets read, addressed and pushed onward. Half a millisecond."
   ACTION: Lights flick, Dex is flung onward.

## SHEET 3 — THE DATA CENTRE (2:00–3:00)

[STYLE BLOCK]

Panels:

1. `2:00-2:08` — **ARRIVAL** — Tiny Dex at a chain-link fence, vast windowless
   shed beyond.
   Caption: "Next stop is what people actually mean when they say 'the cloud.'"
   ACTION: Dex arrives at the fence.
2. `2:08-2:15` — **THE SCALE OF IT** — Extreme wide, flat site, Dex a speck in
   the corner.
   Caption: "A shed the size of a town centre. No windows. No signage."
   ACTION: Camera pulls back.
3. `2:15-2:23` — **ROWS AND ROWS** — Dex walking a cold aisle, racks receding to
   a vanishing point.
   Caption: "Aisles of racks running further away than you can see."
   ACTION: Dex walks, perspective stretches.
4. `2:23-2:30` — **FINDING THE SERVER** — One rack unit lit bright among hundreds
   dark, Dex pointing.
   Caption: "One machine, in one rack, is handling Dex's four words."
   ACTION: Single unit lights up.
5. `2:30-2:38` — **IT LANDS ON A DISK** — Macro: a light pulse striking a
   spinning platter, leaving a mark.
   Caption: "The message becomes a physical mark on a physical disk."
   ACTION: Pulse hits, mark is written.
6. `2:38-2:45` — **THE HEAT** — Giant cooling units, heat shimmer, Dex's hair and
   jacket blown back.
   Caption: "All of this makes heat. Most of the building is fans."
   ACTION: Fans spin up, Dex blown back.
7. `2:45-2:53` — **AND THE POWER** — A rank of outdoor generators, one thumping
   into life with exhaust smoke.
   Caption: "If the power drops for one second, these take over."
   ACTION: One generator kicks in.
8. `2:53-3:00` — **COPIED, AND ONWARD** — A duplicate message bubble spawning and
   rocketing out of frame.
   Caption: "A copy is made. Then the message is thrown at the ocean."
   ACTION: Duplicate spawns and rockets away.

## SHEET 4 — THE OCEAN (3:00–4:00)

[STYLE BLOCK]

Panels:

1. `3:00-3:08` — **TO THE COAST** — Clay relief map, a glowing dashed line
   running from the shed to the shoreline.
   Caption: "Inland fibre runs to the shore, to a building even fewer people notice."
   ACTION: Dashed line draws itself to the coast.
2. `3:08-3:15` — **THE LANDING STATION** — Small plain coastal building, thick
   cables entering the ground beside it.
   Caption: "A cable landing station. Where the internet gets its feet wet."
   ACTION: Camera tracks the cable into the ground.
3. `3:15-3:23` — **INTO THE SEA** — Cable snaking from clay sand into surf, tiny
   Dex riding it under.
   Caption: "The cable leaves the beach, drops off the shelf, and keeps going."
   ACTION: Dex rides the cable under the surface.
4. `3:23-3:30` — **THINNER THAN YOU'D THINK** — Underwater cross-section, Dex
   holding the cable, hose-width.
   Caption: "In deep water the whole thing is about as thick as a garden hose."
   ACTION: Dex holds it up for scale.
5. `3:30-3:38` — **REPEATERS** — A cylindrical repeater on the seabed pulsing
   bright, Dex beside it.
   Caption: "Every sixty miles a repeater grabs the fading light and shouts it on."
   ACTION: Repeater pulses bright.
6. `3:38-3:45` — **THE SEABED ROAD** — Wide dark seabed vista, cable running to
   the horizon over ridges.
   Caption: "It crosses trenches and plains no one has properly mapped."
   ACTION: Slow track along the cable.
7. `3:45-3:53` — **HOW FRAGILE IT IS** — A huge clay ship's anchor dragging
   through silt nearby, Dex flinching.
   Caption: "Anchors cut these. Trawlers cut these. About two hundred times a year."
   ACTION: Anchor drags past, Dex flinches.
8. `3:53-4:00` — **THE FAR SHORE** — Cable rising toward brightening water, far
   shoreline visible above.
   Caption: "But not today. Today it makes it, and the seabed starts climbing."
   ACTION: Cable rises toward the light.

[STYLE BLOCK]

Panels:

   ACTION: Dex rushes along the fibre.
3. `4:15-4:23` — **ANOTHER EXCHANGE** — A second windowless hall, routing lights

   Caption: "Another windowless hall. Another half-millisecond decision."
   ACTION: Routing lights flick.
4. `4:23-4:30` — **LAST TOWER** — Dex rocketing up a mast and launching off the

   Caption: "Grey cabinet. Up the mast. Back into thin air for the last few streets."

5. `4:30-4:38` — **THE PHONE LIGHTS UP** — A clay phone on a table in a sunlit
   room, screen lighting, message landing.
   Caption: "A phone eight thousand miles away buzzes once."
   ACTION: Screen lights, phone buzzes.
6. `4:38-4:45` — **HOW LONG THAT TOOK** — A clay stopwatch snapping to 0.1

   Caption: "Two exchanges, one data centre, one ocean. About a tenth of a second."

7. `4:45-4:53` — **THE REAL MAP** — A clay world map drawing itself with cable

   Caption: "None of it was in the sky. All of it was in the ground and the water."

   Caption: "The cloud is a shed with a postcode and a very large electricity bill."
   ACTION: Cloud icon flips to a building.
